# ZAM Trims Limited — Corporate Website Creation, Development & Maintenance

**Live Website:** https://zamtrims.com/

## Project Overview

Created and maintained the corporate website for **ZAM Trims Limited**, a garment accessories manufacturing company. The website presents the company's services, company profile, certifications, and business information to online visitors and prospective customers.

This repository documents my contribution to the creation, development, ongoing maintenance, and security-related upkeep of the company's business website.

## My Role

**WordPress Website Developer & Website Administrator**
